CEOs, CIOs, CFOs, and operations leaders need to agree on what their AI investment is supposed to accomplish. That means defining where AI can strengthen your competitive position, which opportunities deserve investment, and what governance you need to manage the risks. Only then can you judge the bill against the business value you expect it to deliver.
