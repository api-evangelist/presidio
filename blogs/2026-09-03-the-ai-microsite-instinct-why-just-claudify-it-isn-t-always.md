A microsite built directly from a meeting transcript often becomes too text-heavy and tries to do the job of a presentation deck. Decks are best for scannable information such as scope, objectives, risks, team structure, and commercial details. Microsites add the most value when clients can make live decisions, edit assumptions, test scenarios, or preview deliverables.
