The Gold Standard for AWS: Two Presidio Solutions Architects Earn the Coveted AWS Golden Jacket. In AWS circles there is one award the technical community covets above all others: the AWS Golden Jacket. AWS presents it to individuals who hold every active AWS certification at the same time, 12 in total, spanning the foundational, associate, professional and speciality levels of the AWS certification programme.We are delighted to announce that Will Chalmers and Paul Corley, both solutions architects in Presidio’s UK and Ireland team, have each been awarded the Golden Jacket by AWS.
